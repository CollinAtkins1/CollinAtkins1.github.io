## About Me

I am a senior aerospace engineering student interested in flight dynamics and control, aerodynamics, structures, and spacecraft systems. My projects include MATLAB analysis, experimental comparisons, and aircraft and rocket design.

I currently serve as Stability and Control Lead for our Titan Sea Drone senior design project and am developing an independent rocket project toward NAR Level 1 certification. I am also involved in the Alabama Rocketry Association's IREC team and the University of Alabama AIAA student chapter.

After completing my bachelor's degree, I plan to pursue an M.S. in Aerospace Engineering beginning in Fall 2027.

## Engineering Projects

### [Titan Sea Drone — Stability and Control](projects/titan-drone.html)

**Senior Design · Stability and Control Lead · In Progress**

Developed the preliminary stability and control design plan for a VTOL drone concept intended to fly on Titan and sample its hydrocarbon seas. My work includes a qualitative PID–LQR comparison, subsystem input requirements, risk assessment, and a staged verification plan.

### [Aerospace Structures Analysis](projects/structures.html)

**MATLAB · Beam Theory · Experimental Comparison**

Modeled coupled bending of an L-shaped cantilever beam and compared predictions with laboratory measurements. Fitting the modulus and root boundary conditions reduced vertical displacement RMSE by approximately 91% for the selected dataset.

### [Spacecraft Mission Design Program](projects/spacecraft-mission.html)

**MATLAB · Orbital Mechanics · Numerical Integration**

Developed a program to model a two-stage launch and subsequent orbital maneuvers, including transfer, plane change, and rendezvous. Calculated maneuver requirements and visualized the spacecraft trajectories.

### [NAR Level 1 Certification Rocket](projects/rocket.html)

**OpenRocket · Independent Project · In Progress**

Developed a preliminary H100W-powered rocket design and simulated its stability, trajectory, and parachute recovery. The current model predicts an apogee of 2,737 ft; construction and the certification flight are pending.

### [NACA Airfoil Analysis](projects/airfoils.html)

**XFOIL · Excel · Aerodynamics**

Compared NACA airfoil lift characteristics, pitching moments, and pressure distributions using thin airfoil theory and numerical analysis.

### [Airplane Performance Analysis](projects/airplane-performance.html)

**MATLAB · Aircraft Performance · Numerical Analysis**

Built a reusable program to compare jet and propeller aircraft speed limits, climb performance, sustained turns, range, endurance, and ceiling altitudes under simplified aerodynamic and propulsion assumptions.

### [Glider Design and Flight Testing](projects/glider.html)

**Team Lead · Fabrication · Flight Testing**

Led a four-person team in designing, building, and testing a balsa-wood glider. The longest recorded flight covered 30 ft while carrying four quarters, and the documented project cost was $23.74 against a $50 budget.
